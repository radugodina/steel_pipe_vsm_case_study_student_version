Your task

Identify 2–3 concrete opportunities for digitalization and improvement at this stage. Consider what data should be collected, useful KPIs and how information could support decisions.

Activity for the 1st practical class

1. Understand the process

Reconstruct the flow and identify value-added and non-value-added activities.

2. Identify problems

Look for waiting, inventory, transport, changeovers, quality losses and manual handling.

3. Define data & KPIs

Decide what should be measured at the most relevant stages.

4. Digitalization opportunities

Propose 2–3 specific improvements for the stages you analyse.

5. Connect data to decisions

Explain how collected data could support monitoring and decision-making.

6. A4 synthesis

Summarise process, problems, required data, KPIs, digitalization opportunities and conclusion.
